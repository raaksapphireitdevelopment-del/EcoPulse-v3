9 Configurable Industry Domains

EcoPulse operates on a unified multi-domain engine with customized activity data intake, emission factors, and operational KPIs for each industry sector.

1. Healthcare

Licensed bed-days, anesthetic and medical gases (N2O, Sevoflurane), incinerated biomedical waste, sterilization steam.

2. Manufacturing

Industrial natural gas furnaces, high-voltage plant power, raw steel and polymer supply chain freight.

3. Agriculture

Synthetic nitrogen fertilizer (N2O soil flux), dairy cattle enteric fermentation (CH4), off-road tractor diesel.

4. Energy & Utilities

Gas turbine combustion, high-voltage switchgear SF6 fugitives (GWP 24,300), grid transmission & distribution loss.

5. Transportation & Logistics

Commercial long-haul articulated diesel trucks, delivery fleet vans, EV charging depots, and air freight ton-km.

6. Construction & Real Estate

Embodied carbon in CEM I cement and steel rebar, on-site crane diesel, commercial office HVAC electricity.

7. Waste Management

Landfill fugitive methane emissions, landfill heavy compactor gas oil, MRF sorting electricity, and composting diversion.

8. Hospitality & Tourism

Commercial hotel kitchen LPG, guest room HVAC electricity, industrial laundry steam, and food supply chain.

9. IT & Data Centers

Data center UPS & server rack electricity, PUE monitoring, district chilled water loops, and hyperscale cloud compute.

Integrity Framework

4-Tier Carbon Asset Classification

EcoPulse maintains strict integrity boundaries. Internal operational reductions and early stage project candidates are clearly distinguished from third-party verified environmental assets and official registry-issued carbon credits.

Disclaimer: EcoPulse does not issue credits, operate a registry, or process financial payments. All marketplace listings require accredited third-party verification evidence.
Tier 1: Operational Reduction

Internal energy efficiency, HVAC upgrades, process optimizations, and facility capex reductions tracked against organizational baseline.

Tier 2: Project Candidate

Feasibility stage mitigation projects designed under accredited methodologies (VCS, Gold Standard) awaiting third-party validation.

Tier 3: Verified Environmental Asset

Projects with third-party verification statements, monitored mitigation data, and validated Project Design Documents (PDD).

Tier 4: Issued Carbon Credit

Verified assets with authentic registry serial numbers and issuance documentation from accredited international registries.
